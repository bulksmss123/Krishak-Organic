🌿 About Krishak Organic

Established with a vision to rejuvenate traditional agriculture, Krishak Organic is a farmer-first initiative based in Kishangarh, Khairthal district, Alwar, Rajasthan. We work closely with local farming communities to promote organic inputs, regenerative practices, and fair market access.

Our commitment to "Agriculture & Allied Activities" includes end-to-end support — from soil health management and organic certification assistance to post-harvest handling. We believe in producing nutrient-rich food while preserving biodiversity and empowering rural livelihoods.
